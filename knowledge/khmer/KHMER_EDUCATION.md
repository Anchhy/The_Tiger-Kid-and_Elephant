# Khmer Educational Design Guidelines (Phase 3)

> **Status**: Educational design framework for Cambodian primary-school learners. All draft Khmer hints and example sentences must be reviewed by a native Khmer teacher before production release.

---

## 1. Target Learner Considerations

- **Audience**: Cambodian children learning foundational Khmer literacy, vocabulary recognition, and word construction alongside the game *The Tiger, Kid, and Elephant*.
- **Pedagogical Goals**:
  1. Connect visual concepts directly to accurate Khmer spellings (**Picture Matching**).
  2. Strengthen Khmer-to-meaning association and attention to detail (**Word Matching**).
  3. Build confidence recognizing valid Khmer syllable segments (**Syllable Ordering**).
- **Language Tone**: Warm, encouraging, concise, and free of complex grammatical jargon.

---

## 2. Three-Tier Educational Hint Design

Hints should scaffold a child's thinking without giving away the answer on the first tap.

| Hint Level | Purpose | Easy Example (`ដំរី` — Elephant) | Guidelines |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **`easy` (Hint 1)** | Broad category or simple everyday cue | *Draft EN*: "It is a large animal."<br>*Draft KM*: "វាជាសត្វធំមួយប្រភេទ។" *(needs review)* | Never include the target Khmer word or direct translation. |
| **`medium` (Hint 2)** | Appearance, function, or habitat clue | *Draft EN*: "It has a long trunk and big ears."<br>*Draft KM*: "វាមានប្រមោយវែង និងត្រចៀកធំ។" *(needs review)* | Give a concrete visual or functional attribute children know. |
| **`strong` (Hint 3)** | Strong structural/story clue without full reveal | *Draft EN*: "It waits at the end of the maze and has 2 syllables starting with ដំ."<br>*Draft KM*: "សត្វនេះរង់ចាំនៅទីបញ្ចប់ ហើយផ្តើមដោយព្យាង្គ «ដំ»។" *(needs review)* | For Syllable Ordering, reveal the first syllable or meaning; for Hard mode, restrict hints per difficulty rules. |

---

## 3. Natural Example Sentence Principles

1. **Natural Khmer Syntax**: Follow standard Khmer `Subject + Verb + Object` and post-nominal modifier order (`ផ្ទះធំ`, not English *"ធំផ្ទះ"*).
2. **Short & Child-Friendly**: Keep sentences to 4–8 words with familiar everyday verbs (`មាន`, `ទៅ`, `រៀន`, `ញ៉ាំ`, `ស្រឡាញ់`).
3. **Appropriate Register**: Use polite, natural child/everyday vocabulary (e.g., `ញ៉ាំ` / `ហូប` rather than `ស៊ី` for people; `ស៊ី` only when referring to animals).
4. **No Mechanical Translation**: Never translate English articles (*a/the*) or copulas (*is/are*) word-for-word into unnatural Khmer.
5. **Mandatory Review Flag**: Every newly authored `example_km` and `definition_km` starts with `"example": "needs_review"` until checked by a native Khmer teacher.

---

## 4. Recommended Learning Sequence

1. **Stage 1 (Starter / Easy)**: Story-linked animals & immediate daily nouns (`ខ្លា`, `ដំរី`, `កូនក្មេង`, `ទឹក`, `បាយ`, `ផ្ទះ`, `ផ្កា`).
2. **Stage 2 (Core / Easy–Medium)**: Family, common foods, and nature (`ឪពុក`, `ម្តាយ`, `ផ្លែឈើ`, `ដើមឈើ`, `សៀវភៅ`, `ប៊ិច`, `តុ`).
3. **Stage 3 (Expansion / Medium–Hard)**: Multi-syllable words, compounds, and school/community vocabulary (`សាលារៀន`, `គ្រូបង្រៀន`, `មេអំបៅ`, `កសិករ`, `បរិស្ថាន`).

---

## 5. Age-Appropriate Content Standards

- Avoid frightening, violent, or culturally insensitive examples (even for `ខ្លា` tiger, frame hints/sentences around forest wildlife or the maze story rather than harm).
- Prefer concrete, observable nouns and daily school/family contexts familiar across urban and rural Cambodia (e.g., `បាយ`, `ទន្លេ`, `កសិករ`, `សាលារៀន`).
- Keep Khmer text uncrowded and legible on mobile screens, especially for words with stacked subscripts (`ជើងអក្សរ`).
