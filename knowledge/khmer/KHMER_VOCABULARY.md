## 1. Principles for Defining Khmer Vocabulary

A Khmer word cannot be defined reliably by a 1-to-1 English translation. Every entry in the knowledge base should account for:

1. **Khmer Spelling (`khmer`)**: Exact canonical Unicode form (preserving subscripts and diacritics).
2. **Core & Contextual Meanings (`english`, `definition_en`)**: Capture primary child-friendly meaning while noting polysemy when relevant (e.g., `បាយ` means both *cooked rice* and *a meal*).
3. **Simple Khmer Definition (`definition_km`)**: Short, natural Khmer phrasing suitable for primary learners (never word-for-word English translations; requires native review).
4. **Part of Speech (`part_of_speech`)**: Based on Khmer grammatical behavior rather than forcing English categories.
5. **Context & Register**: Specify social register (everyday, polite/formal, animal-only, literary) and literal vs. figurative usage.

## 2. Khmer Parts of Speech (`ថ្នាក់ពាក្យ`)

Khmer is an analytic/isolating language (with derivational prefixes/infixes), using word order and particles rather than inflectional endings for tense or plural.

| Category | Khmer Term | Grammatical Behavior | Real Khmer Examples |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Noun** | `នាម` | Names people, animals, objects, places; does not inflect for plural. | `ដំរី` (elephant), `សៀវភៅ` (book), `ផ្ទះ` (house) |
| **Verb** | `កិរិយាសព្ទ` | Expresses actions/events; unmarked for tense (aspect particles express completion/time). | `រៀន` (study), `ញ៉ាំ` / `ហូប` (eat), `ដើរ` (walk) |
| **Stative Verb (Adjective)** | `គុណនាម` / `កិរិយាសព្ទសភាព` | **No separate English-style adjective class**: quality words act directly as predicates without a copula ("is/are") and follow nouns when modifying them. | `ផ្ទះធំ` (big house), `ផ្ទះនេះល្អ` (this house [is] good) |
| **Adverb** | `គុណកិរិយា` | Modifies verbs or stative verbs (manner, degree, time), typically following the word it modifies. | `លឿន` (fast), `ណាស់` (very), `ឥឡូវ` (now) |
| **Pronoun** | `សព្វនាម` | Strongly governed by age, kinship, and social register rather than neutral "I/you". | `ខ្ញុំ` (I — polite), `គាត់` (he/she — respectful), `បង` / `ប្អូន` (older/younger) |
| **Classifier** | `រនាប់នាម` / `ពាក្យរាប់` | Required when counting nouns (`Noun + Number + Classifier`); chosen by shape/category. | `គោ ៣ ក្បាល` (3 cows — `ក្បាល` for animals), `ផ្កា ៥ ទង` / `ផ្លែប៉ោម ៥ ផ្លែ`, `ដើមឈើ ២ ដើម` |
| **Particle & Marker** | `និបាត` / `ឧបបទ` | Placed at clause edges to mark questions, negation, aspect, or politeness. | `ទេ` (negation / yes-no question), `ហើយ` (already / and then), `ផង` (also / please) |

## 4. Semantic Knowledge & Lexical Relationships

### A. Polysemy & Multiple Meanings
- **`បាយ`**: Literal *cooked rice*, extended to *a meal* (`ញ៉ាំបាយ` = eat a meal).
- **`ផ្លែ`**: Means *fruit* (`ផ្លែឈើ`), and also serves as the *classifier* for fruits and bladed objects.
- **`ដើម`**: Means *origin/trunk/plant* (`ដើមឈើ` = tree) and serves as the *classifier* for trees and pole-like items.

### B. English-to-Khmer Lexical Splits (Avoiding Mechanical Translation)
1. **"Rice" (Stage differentiation)**:
   - `ស្រូវ` = paddy / unhusked rice growing in the field
   - `អង្ករ` = husked raw rice grains
   - `បាយ` = cooked rice / meal
2. **"Eat" (Social register differentiation)**:
   - `ស៊ី` = animals / rough informal
   - `ហូប` / `ញ៉ាំ` = standard everyday polite/casual for people (`ញ៉ាំ` common in modern speech)
   - `ពិសា` = formal/respectful (elders, guests, formal writing)
3. **"Carry" (Physical posture differentiation)**:
   - `កាន់` = hold/carry in the hand
   - `យួរ` = carry dangling by a handle/strap
   - `លី` = carry on the shoulder
   - `ពរ` = cradle in the arms (e.g., a baby)

### C. Synonyms, Antonyms, Related Words & Visual Confusables
- **Synonyms / Register Pairs**: `ហូប` ~ `ញ៉ាំ` (eat — casual/everyday), `កូនក្មេង` ~ `កុមារ` (child — everyday vs. formal).
- **Antonyms**: `ធំ` (big) ↔ `តូច` (small), `បង` (older sibling) ↔ `ប្អូន` (younger sibling), `ពេលព្រឹក` (morning) ↔ `ពេលយប់` (night).
- **Related Words (Thematic Pairs)**: `គ្រូបង្រៀន` (teacher) ↔ `សិស្ស` (student) ↔ `សាលារៀន` (school); `ភ្លៀង` (rain) ↔ `មេឃ` (sky) ↔ `ទន្លេ` (river).
- **Visually Similar Words (Puzzle Distractor Caution)**:
  - `ដ` vs. `ត` subscripts (`្ដ` vs. `្ត`), e.g., `ម្តាយ` vs. non-words.
  - `ប៉` vs. `ប៊` register shifters.
  - Words sharing root morphemes (`ដើមឈើ` tree vs. `ផ្លែឈើ` fruit vs. `ព្រៃឈើ` forest) make strong **Medium/Hard** distractors but should be avoided together in **Easy** Picture Matching.

## 5. Difficulty Framework (`easy`, `medium`, `hard`)

Difficulty is **not** determined by word length alone. It combines 6 criteria:

| Criterion | Easy | Medium | Hard |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Child Familiarity** | Immediate home/animal/food words (`ខ្លា`, `បាយ`, `ផ្ទះ`). | Familiar school/family/nature words (`សៀវភៅ`, `ដើមឈើ`, `ទន្លេ`). | Abstract/broader community or multi-part concepts (`បរិស្ថាន`, `កសិករ`, `នាឡិកា`). |
| **Orthographic & Syllable Structure** | Simple 1–2 unit words without complex multi-syllable loan clusters (`ដំរី`, `ទឹក`). | 2-syllable words or simple compounds (`ផ្លែឈើ`, `ឪពុក`, `កៅអី`). | 3-syllable words, compounds, or Sanskrit/Pali-derived spellings (`សាលារៀន`, `ព្រះអាទិត្យ`). |
| **Meaning Complexity** | Concrete single referent. | Compound or relational meaning (`ប្អូន`, `ព្រៃឈើ`). | Conceptual/categorical meaning (`បរិស្ថាន`, `គ្រូបង្រៀន`). |
| **Picture Clarity** | Unambiguous standalone object/animal illustration. | Clear scene/object (`ភ្លៀង`, `ផ្សារ`). | Abstract or context-dependent illustration (`បរិស្ថាន`, `សាលារៀន`). |
